ProxyNaut Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 17, 2026

ProxyNaut is a network utility tool. We value your privacy and this policy outlines how we handle user data.

1. Data Collection

ProxyNaut does not collect any personal information. We do not require registration, nor do we request your name, email, phone number, or any biological data.

2. Traffic Processing

As a network proxy tool, all traffic processing occurs locally on your device. We do not operate relay servers and do not intercept, read, or log your browsing history or communication content.

3. Local Storage

Your server configurations and subscription links are stored exclusively within the app's protected sandbox on your device. This data is never uploaded to any external server or developer cloud.

4. Third-Party SDKs

The app does not integrate any third-party ads, tracking, or analysis plugins (such as Google Analytics), ensuring your activities remain entirely private.
