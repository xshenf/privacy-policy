Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I change the language?

Go to the Settings page within the app and select your preferred language (English, Chinese, Japanese, or Korean).

Does the app need internet?

No, ScoreView works entirely offline. No data is sent to any servers.

Why is there no sound?

Ensure your device's volume is up and the "Silent Mode" switch on your iPhone is turned off. If problems persist, try restarting the app.
