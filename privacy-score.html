Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

At ScoreView, we protect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled.

1. We Do Not Collect Any Personal Information

ScoreView is a local-only application. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to our servers.

2. No Data Sharing

Since no data is collected, no data is shared with third parties.

3. Permissions

  • Audio Output: The app generates sound locally using the Web Audio API.
  • Keep Awake: The app may request to keep your screen on while practicing.
  • Haptics: The app uses haptic feedback for certain interactions.

None of these permissions involve the collection of personal data.

4. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information, ScoreView is safe for users of all ages.
