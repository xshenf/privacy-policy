Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Expiry Hub ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Expiry Hub mobile application (the "App").

Summary

Expiry Hub is a local-first app. We do not operate any servers, do not collect analytics, and do not track you.

Your data stays on your device and, if you enable it, in your personal iCloud account.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Expiry Hub does not transmit your data to any third-party servers. All item data, receipt photos, reminders, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Camera — Used solely to capture receipt photos for OCR (optical character recognition) within the App. Photos are stored locally on your device.
  • Photo Library — Used solely to attach existing photos from your library as receipt images. We do not access or read your photo library beyond the photos you explicitly select.

All permissions are optional. The App functions fully without granting any permissions; they only enhance specific features.

iCloud Sync

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your data is synced through Apple's CloudKit framework directly between your devices using your personal Apple ID. We do not have access to your iCloud data at any point. Synced data is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Expiry Hub offers optional Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases through Apple's StoreKit framework. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, credit card numbers, or Apple ID credentials. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles your payment data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection SDKs. The only external service involved is Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), used exclusively for optional data sync between your devices.

Text Recognition (OCR)

Receipt text recognition is performed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No images or recognized text are sent to external servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

All your data is stored locally on your device. You can delete any item, receipt, or all data at any time within the App. Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data. If iCloud sync is enabled, synced data in your iCloud account may persist according to Apple's policies and can be managed through your device's iCloud settings.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not require users to create accounts or provide personal details of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
