Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 3, 2026

At Screen Color, we take your privacy very seriously. This policy explains how we handle data within our application.

1. Data Collection

Screen Color is designed as a standalone utility. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or visual imagery.

2. Camera Usage

  • Local Processing: The app requires camera access solely to analyze light spectrum and screen color temperature. All processing is performed strictly on your local device.
  • No Storage: We do not record, save, or upload any photos, videos, or visual stream data. Camera frames are used in-memory for calculation and discarded immediately.

3. Transparency

The app does not use third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks. Your data never leaves your device.
