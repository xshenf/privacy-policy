Introduction

Welcome to BrainParallel. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Our Promise: Your data stays on your device. We don't see it. We can't sell it.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

BrainParallel stores your data locally on your device using Apple's standard storage mechanisms. This may include:

  • Game progress, level unlocks, and star ratings
  • Training history and session scores
  • Daily streak and achievement records
  • App preferences (sound, haptics, guide lines, etc.)
  • Premium purchase status (via Apple StoreKit)
Important: All data is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded to any server.

Analytics

BrainParallel does not collect analytics data. The app runs entirely on your device, and no usage information is sent to any server.

In-App Purchases

BrainParallel offers a one-time, lifetime purchase that unlocks all premium features. There are no subscriptions, no recurring charges, and no automatic renewals. Payment is processed once through Apple's App Store.

Purchase status is managed by Apple StoreKit and stored on your device. We do not collect or store any payment information — all billing is handled securely by Apple.

Your Rights and Choices

Access and Control

  • View Data: All your data is accessible within the app
  • Delete Data: Use the in-app "Reset Progress" option in Settings, or delete the app
  • Restore Purchases: Use the "Restore Purchases" button on the paywall or in Settings

Children's Privacy

BrainParallel does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
