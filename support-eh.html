Frequently Asked Questions

What is Expiry Hub?

Expiry Hub is an all-in-one tracker for items with expiration dates — subscriptions, warranties, IDs, gift cards, refund windows, and more. Keep everything organized in one place with timely reminders.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is also synced to your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. We never store your data on our servers.

How do I enable iCloud sync?

Open Expiry Hub → Settings → toggle on iCloud Sync. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID on all devices you want to sync.

How do receipt photos and OCR work?

You can attach a receipt photo when adding or editing an item. Expiry Hub uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to recognize text from the photo. All processing happens locally on your device — no images are uploaded anywhere.

What do I get with the Pro upgrade?

Pro unlocks advanced features including custom categories, iCloud sync across devices, receipt photo attachments with OCR, and enhanced reminder options. Available as monthly, yearly, or lifetime plans with a 7-day free trial.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad to view, modify, or cancel your subscription.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Expiry Hub → Settings → tap Restore Purchases. This will restore any previously purchased Pro subscription or lifetime unlock on the same Apple ID.

How do I delete all my data?

You can delete individual items from within the App. To remove all data, uninstall the app from your device. If iCloud sync was enabled, you can also remove synced data through your device's iCloud settings.

Does Expiry Hub collect my personal information?

No. Expiry Hub is a local-first app with no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party data collection. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
